# Brain Gym — Rules

_Brain Gym is a single-player daily brain workout. The rules document is the
authoritative source of truth. If the implementation ever diverges from this
document, fix the implementation — never silently modify the rules._

## 1. Objective

Complete one full workout: all three brain exercises — Memory Matrix,
Quick Tap and Color Clash — in order, and score as many points as possible.
Your three exercise scores combine into one **Brain Score**. Beat your
all-time best, and beat today's Daily Challenge best.

## 2. Setup

- A workout consists of exactly three mini-games, always in this order:
  1. 🔢 Memory Matrix
  2. ⚡ Quick Tap
  3. 🎨 Color Clash
- Each mini-game has its own difficulty tier (Easy / Medium / Hard), chosen
  on the menu before the workout starts.
- The Daily Challenge uses Medium difficulty for all three mini-games and a
  deterministic seed derived from the calendar date, so every player gets
  the same workout all day.
- Every mini-game begins with a 3-2-1-GO countdown; rounds start after "GO".

## 3. Turn order

- Mini-games run in fixed order. Within a mini-game, rounds run in
  sequence: round *n* must fully resolve before round *n+1* begins.
- After the last round of a mini-game, a mini-summary screen shows the
  exercise score, then the next mini-game's countdown begins.
- After the third mini-game's summary, the workout-done screen shows the
  total Brain Score, per-exercise breakdowns, and records statistics.
- The player may pause (freezing all timers), restart the whole workout,
  or quit to the menu at any time via the pause button.

## 4. Legal moves

- **Memory Matrix — recall phase:** tap any face-down tile. Tapping a target
  tile flips it face-up (state "found").
- **Quick Tap — green phase:** tap the pad exactly once when it turns green.
- **Color Clash — prompt phase:** tap the color button whose color matches
  the INK of the displayed word (not the word's text).

## 5. Illegal moves

- Taps during non-input phases (countdown, reveal/show phase, rest,
  resolve, summary, done) are ignored — they never change state.
- Tapping an already-found memory tile is ignored.
- Tapping a Stroop button after the round resolved is ignored.
- An invalid tap can never crash, freeze, or desync the engine.

## 6. Captures

Not applicable — Brain Gym is a single-player scoring workout with no
pieces, no opponent, and no captures.

## 7. Special rules

- **Quick Tap false start:** tapping while the pad is red (waiting) adds a
  fixed **+1500ms penalty** to that round's time and ends the round.
- **Memory Matrix miss:** tapping a tile that is not a target immediately
  ends the round, briefly reveals the missed targets, and moves on.
- **Color Clash mismatch bias:** 75% of the time the word text differs from
  its ink color (incongruent); otherwise it may match.
- **Color Clash time limit:** on Medium each word must be answered within
  5000ms; on Hard within 3000ms. On Easy there is no time limit. A timeout
  counts as a wrong answer.
- **Daily Challenge:** deterministic (same seed all day, Medium for all
  exercises); may be replayed to improve today's best.

## 8. Scoring

- **Memory Matrix:** each perfectly recalled round scores
  `targets × 20` points (e.g. 6 tiles = 120). A missed round scores 0.
- **Quick Tap:** score = `((1500 − average_ms) clamped to 0…1500) ÷ 3`
  (integer division). A false start counts 1500ms for that round.
- **Color Clash:** score = `(correct × 60 − wrong × 30) clamped to 0…900`.
- **Brain Score** = Memory + Reaction + Stroop totals.
- A new all-time best is recorded whenever the Brain Score strictly exceeds
  the previous best. Daily bests are recorded per calendar date.

## 9. Winning conditions

Brain Gym has no win/lose in the competitive sense. A workout is
successfully completed when all three exercises finish. Personal victory
= new all-time best (celebrated with a "NEW BEST!" banner), or a new
Daily Challenge best.

## 10. Draw conditions

Not applicable — single-player scoring game.

## 11. AI strategy

Not applicable — no AI opponent. The Daily Challenge's workout content is
generated by a seeded PRNG (`Random(dateSeed)`), guaranteeing the same
sequence of tiles, waits and Stroop words for every player on a given day.

## 12. Edge cases

- All scores clamp at zero; negative totals are impossible.
- Memory Matrix always shows between `tilesMin` and `tilesMax` targets
  (≥ 3), so an empty board is impossible.
- Quick Tap always records one time per round (measured or 1500ms
  penalty), so an average always exists.
- Color Clash button order is fixed (RED, BLUE, GREEN, YELLOW).
- Pausing mid-round freezes the engine's phase timer; resuming re-arms the
  current phase. App backgrounding freezes the engine; foregrounding
  unfreezes it (unless the explicit pause menu is open).
- The engine watchdog re-arms any timed phase found without a live timer,
  so a stuck state is impossible by construction.

## 13. Test cases

1. Perfect memory round with *k* tiles scores exactly *k × 20*.
2. A wrong memory tile ends the round, flashes the targets, scores 0 for
   the round.
3. Tapping the red Quick Tap pad records 1500ms and shows the penalty
   banner; the next round still starts.
4. Tapping the green pad records the elapsed ms; score formula matches §8.
5. Correct Stroop tap (ink color) increments correct; wrong tap increments
   wrong; timeout on Medium/Hard counts wrong.
6. After the last round of an exercise, the summary shows the exact
   exercise score; after the third exercise, the done screen shows
   Brain Score = sum of the three.
7. New best sets the "NEW BEST!" banner and updates the stored best.
8. Daily Challenge twice on the same date produces the same seed content.
9. Rename: type a name → the profile shows it immediately and it persists
   across app restarts in original order.
10. Pause → restart resets all three exercise scores to 0 and restarts the
    countdown of the first exercise.
11. Backgrounding mid-round and foregrounding resumes without a frozen
    state and without lost timers.
12. Pro purchase (when products exist in Play Console) unlocks Hard
    difficulty, all 12 themes, all 10 tile styles and the custom theme
    creator; without store products the Pro screen shows the honest
    "available after store setup" state.
